The problem

Your followers want to weigh in. The apps make it hard.

  • Instagram, TikTok and Facebook each count their own votes. You never get one answer.
  • The algorithm decides who sees your question, not you.
  • When it's over, the result is gone.

Where it's headed

From popular to trusted.

Grid is Zzzap's next big step. Zzzap will learn what each question is about and who answers it well.

  • Expert AsksPeople on Zzzap can ask a vetted expert in a topic, like golf or fitness. That expert is you.
  • Known for good adviceNot follower count. Grid notices when your advice actually helps, and you earn topic badges you can share.
  • Never a leaderboardNo ranking against anyone.
  • You stay in chargePick your topic and how many asks you take a day.

Surge gets you heard. Grid gets you known as the person to ask.
