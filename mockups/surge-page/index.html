Surge page mockups

Three ways to keep the photo still while the information scrolls up over it. Open each on your iPhone, scroll, and tap an answer to see the results. Sample data.

A. Glass Sheet

Top half is the photo. A frosted sheet starts halfway down and slides up over it like an iPhone sheet; the photo dims and softens behind it.

 B. Magazine

Big type right on the photo, like a cover, with the live question and a Vote button. Scrolling fades the photo to black and the rest reads like an article: answers on hairlines, the bio as a quote, numbered past zzzaps.

 C. Floating Cards

Like an artist page in Apple Music. Name and a Vote button on the photo. Scrolling melts the photo into a blur of its own colors, with the information in separate glass cards floating over it.
